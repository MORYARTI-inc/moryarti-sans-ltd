Moryarti Sans Limited is a display typeface drawn from the logotype of MORYARTI inc., a creative design studio in Asakusa, Tokyo — a district of craftsmen and small workshops. The studio's motto is the idea that the brighter the light, the deeper the shadow, and the typeface is built on it.

Its capitals are heavy and geometric: straight stems, diagonals cut at the same angle throughout, round letters drawn from near-perfect circles. In a number of letters and figures, a stroke dissolves into a printed halftone exactly where a neighbouring stroke would cast its shadow. The light always comes from the same fixed angles and heights, so the shadows stay consistent across a line of text, the way they would on letters cut from card and lit on a table.

The design is capitals only; lowercase input sets the same capital forms, so text can be typed in any case and keep one appearance. The halftone figures 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5 are the default; the stylistic set ss01 gives solid versions of 1, 3 and 5 for tables and longer runs of numbers. Moryarti Sans Limited is intended for headlines, posters, signage and branding at large sizes.
