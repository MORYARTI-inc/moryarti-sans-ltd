https://github.com/MORYARTI-inc/moryarti-sans-ltd

**Super short description of the Font Family:**

Moryarti Sans Limited is a display typeface drawn from the logotype of MORYARTI inc., a creative design studio in
Asakusa, Tokyo: heavy geometric capitals in which a stroke dissolves into a printed halftone where a neighbouring stroke
would cast its shadow. Capitals only (lowercase sets the same forms), one weight; `ss01` gives solid figures 1, 3 and 5.

**A note on the family name:** the family is named after the studio that drew it, MORYARTI inc., which is also the
copyright holder. If "Moryarti" in the name is a problem under the naming guidelines, we would like to discuss it and are
ready to choose another name.

**Use of AI tools:** the letterforms of Moryarti Sans Limited were designed by KENT MORI. AI tools (Anthropic's Claude)
were used only in two places: to add accented letters (umlauts and the other diacritic forms of the Latin Core set),
composed from the designer's own base glyphs and marks; and to prepare this repository for Google Fonts — the build
configuration, font metadata, vertical metrics, documentation and checks. Every glyph was reviewed and approved by the
designer.
